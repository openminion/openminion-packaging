# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive project.

Expected behavior includes professional discussion, good-faith review, focused
collaboration, and respectful disagreement. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not accepted.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and related project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict
participation when necessary to enforce it.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers or request a private reporting
channel without posting sensitive details publicly.
